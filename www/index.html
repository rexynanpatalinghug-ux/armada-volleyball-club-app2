How to Use

1. Enter scores in the blue score fields.

2. A winner is recorded only when a team reaches at least 25 points and leads by at least 2 (for example, 25–23 or 27–25).

3. Winner and loser update automatically.

4. Standings automatically tally wins, losses, points for/against, point differential, matches played, and rank.

5. Ranking: most wins, then highest points scored. Teams tied on both may share a rank.

6. Results are saved on this device automatically. Use Export results (CSV) to save/share a copy.

This app works offline after installation. Data is stored locally on this device; clearing app data will remove saved scores.
